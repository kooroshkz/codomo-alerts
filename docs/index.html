Know the moment a Codomo home is listed.

Good homes go in minutes. This watches the new listings on codomo.eu (formerly Holland2Stay) for you, in the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal and Austria, and messages your phone when one matches your search: your cities, your size, your budget.

Start in a minute

Docker

docker run -d --name codomo-alerts --restart unless-stopped \
  -e CODOMO_CITIES="Eindhoven, Rotterdam" \
  -e CODOMO_PRICE_MAX=1600 \
  -e CODOMO_NOTIFY="tgram://BOT_TOKEN/CHAT_ID" \
  -v codomo-data:/data -p 127.0.0.1:8080:8080 \
  ghcr.io/kooroshkz/codomo-alerts

Dashboard on localhost:8080. Runs on a Raspberry Pi too.

Python

pipx install codomo-alerts
codomo-alerts init
codomo-alerts watch

init asks a few questions and sends a test message.

Looking in Granada, Lisbon or Linz? Use CODOMO_CITIES=Granada, Lisbon or Linz. Add CODOMO_GENDER=female or male to skip rooms Codomo marks for the other gender only. Leave the cities empty for every country, or set CODOMO_COUNTRIES=nl for one.

What you get

Your search

Countries, cities, bedrooms, budget, contract length, furnishing, move-in date, and female only or male only rooms.

Only what's new

New homes, homes back on the market, shortlist homes that turn bookable, price drops. Never the same one twice.

Register interest

Homes where Codomo picks from a shortlist, with how many people already signed up.

New buildings

A message when a new building page shows up on codomo.eu.

Dashboard

What's available, what got taken and how fast, and the hours homes usually show up.

Polite by design

A check every few minutes, faster in the hours homes are released. It says who it is and backs off when asked.

Questions

Is there a Codomo app that tells me about new homes?

Codomo uses the home search in your tenant portal for register-interest homes. For a message about every new listing, filtered your way and sent to the app you already use, run this free, unofficial tool.

How do I get Holland2Stay notifications now?

Holland2Stay became Codomo on 16 November 2026 and holland2stay.com now redirects to codomo.eu. Tools that used the old Holland2Stay API stopped working. This tool was called holland2stay-alerts and reads codomo.eu since version 0.2.0, and your city names still work.

Does it work in Granada, Lisbon and Linz?

Yes. Set CODOMO_CITIES to Granada, Lisbon (Carcavelos) or Linz. Some rooms there are shared, and some are female only or male only: set CODOMO_GENDER to skip the rooms that aren't open to you.

Does it book homes for me?

No. It tells you, you book on codomo.eu yourself. Automated booking is against their terms. Keep a browser tab open and stay logged in so you're one click away.

How fast are the alerts?

Usually within two minutes during weekday release hours, a few minutes the rest of the day. Cheap homes in big cities can still go within a minute, so be ready to act.

What happened to the lottery?

Codomo replaced it with "Register interest": you sign up for a home and Codomo picks someone from the shortlist, using your search profile. It is not first come, first served. You get a message when such a home matches your search, with how many people already showed interest.

Do I need a server?

No. Any computer that stays on works: a laptop on power, a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a small VPS.
